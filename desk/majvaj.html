Coins the bot trades

-

BTC and ETH always stay: the model reads them as reference markets for every coin, so adding or removing another coin never retrains the rest. A new coin needs enough Kraken history (checked when you apply), trains its own model first, and cannot place a trade, paper or live, until that model is ready. Removing a coin never sells it: an open trade on it is managed to its normal exit. The bot still holds at most 3 trades at a time.

Training data: Kraken candle history

-

The full Kraken history is kept in a database on this server; updating only adds what is new. The bot keeps its saved model between restarts and retrains it in the background on schedule.

Outside data (stored point-in-time)

-

Fear & Greed and the FOMC calendar need no key; a FRED key (Keys tab) adds the CPI / jobs / PPI calendar and first-release macro values. Each value is used only after the moment it became public. The bot takes no new trades from 60 minutes before to 90 minutes after these events.

Backtest

Replays the real bot (FreqAI model, money rules, calendar gate, fees) over past candles. Grok's verdicts cannot be replayed honestly, so the backtest runs without them. It runs in the background on 1 CPU with its own model folder; the live bot keeps trading.

When Grok checks the fundamentals

Defaults: 00:00 Tokyo open, 07:00 London open, 13:30 New York pre-open / London overlap. Each verdict lasts until the next check plus one hour; if a check fails, the bot stands down when the old verdict runs out. Scout now (Desk tab) runs one immediately.

Grok model

Each check searches the web and X for news, scheduled events and sentiment, then turns that into NORMAL, CAUTION (half-size trades) or STAND DOWN (no new trades).

Before going live (advice)

    These are recommendations, not a lock: the decision is yours. Live needs only a trade-enabled Kraken key in use (Keys tab), the warning acknowledged and the typed phrase. Real orders use your real Kraken balance, capped by the bankroll below.

    Trading on / off

    -

    Asks for a typed phrase, so a stray click never changes anything. Pausing stops new trades only; open trades keep being managed. Kept across restarts.

    Money rules

    Sessions:

    Winning trades' net profit is protected and never traded again; losses reduce the active bankroll. Open trades are always managed, whatever these settings say. Opportunity mode, after the goal: one extra trade at a time, only in the ticked sessions, smaller, with a stronger signal and a NORMAL news verdict, sized so a loss can't take back more than you chose to give back.

    Restart

    Click twice to confirm. Open trades and their stops are kept in the bot's database and picked up again. The bot is back in about a minute (longer if a coin is still training); at most a few restarts per hour.
